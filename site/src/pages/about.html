About

── What Lunarc is

Lunarc is based on Arch Linux, with the Hyprland window manager and the Noctalia shell. It uses Arch's packages and repositories as they are, and adds its own packages for the desktop setup, the lunarc command, the installer and a few things Arch doesn't carry.
