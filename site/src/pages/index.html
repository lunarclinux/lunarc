Lunarc

Arch Linux with the Hyprland window manager, set up as a complete desktop. The disk is encrypted by default, a snapshot is taken before every update, and nothing about you is sent anywhere.

── What's included

  • ◆Desktop

    Hyprland with the Noctalia shell: a bar, notifications, quick settings, a lock screen, and one menu (SUPER+Space) for apps, settings, installs and updates. Every shortcut is listed and can be changed.

  • +Apps

    Firefox, Files, Text Editor, Papers, Image Viewer, video and music players, Camera, Calculator, Disks, Print Settings, Pika Backup and the Ghostty terminal. Common video and audio formats play out of the box. More from the app catalog: Steam, LibreOffice, Obsidian, VS Code, Signal and others.

  • ■Encrypted

    Full-disk encryption (LUKS2) is the installer's default. The firewall refuses incoming connections. Backups are encrypted.

  • ↑Snapshots

    A snapshot of the system before and after every update. Roll back with one command, or start a snapshot from the boot menu if an update stops the computer from starting.

  • !Privacy

    No telemetry from Lunarc or from the apps it includes. Every connection a fresh install makes is listed, with how to turn it off.

  • ◐Themes

    Seventeen themes, most with dark and light variants. Small or rounded corners, blur on or off.

  • ~Next to Windows

    Installs into the free space of a disk that has Windows on it. Nothing on the Windows partitions is changed; the boot menu has both.

  • ?One command

    lunarc update, lunarc app install, lunarc theme set, lunarc display, lunarc privacy and more. All commands.
